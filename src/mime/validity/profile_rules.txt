# The profile rules: for each profile and class of finding (finding_classes.txt), what the build does.
#
#   generate  a waiver with a pending basis covers the finding, generated where the experiment declares none; it is
#             recorded in the saved form and stamped on every output
#   basis     the finding is covered only by the experiment's own waiver or source with a basis; a pending basis is
#             refused by name, and none is refused as before profiles
#   list      the finding is listed, in the build report and by `mime pending`, and never refused
#   refuse    the finding is refused
#
# A profile and class with no row: nothing (design for every profile; a physical value's missing source in
# exploratory). Profiles act on waivers alone: a rule generates or accepts a waiver, and nothing else, which a test
# holds for every row. `strict` has no rows in dev1 and is refused by name until a1 writes them.
exploratory  assumption         generate
research     assumption         basis
exploratory  pending            generate
research     pending            refuse
exploratory  numerical_default  generate
research     numerical_default  refuse
research     unsourced          list
research     unevidenced        list
